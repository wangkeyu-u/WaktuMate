# Product design

## Users

People in Malaysia who want a respectful mobile utility for daily religious practice, especially Muslims using prayer times, mosque navigation, Qibla direction, zikr counting, and reminders. The app should also support users from other mainstream faiths with a first-run profile and appropriate reference/timer tools without pretending every faith has the same workflows.

## Product Purpose

WaktuMate Malaysia helps users choose a faith profile, access relevant daily tools, and keep lightweight local practice data. Success means the app launches cleanly, feels calm and trustworthy, and makes common actions like checking prayer time, finding a nearby mosque, opening a compass, starting a timer, or opening reference material fast.

## Brand Personality

Calm, respectful, practical. The tone should be helpful and grounded, with enough Malaysian local context to feel specific without becoming visually loud.

## Anti-references

The app should not look like a generic todo app, a busy social/religion portal, a casino-like gamified tracker, or a decorative landing page. Avoid overextended religion-specific assumptions for non-Islam profiles.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the user's current practice context, not promotional copy.
2. Keep religious tools respectful, direct, and low-noise.
3. Make profile-specific differences explicit so users understand why a tool appears.
4. Prefer local-first behavior and graceful fallback when permissions or network fail.
5. Use familiar iOS controls and SF Symbols over invented interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type-friendly layouts where practical, clear contrast in light and dark mode, reduced reliance on color alone, and standard iOS permission flows. Profile wording should be inclusive and avoid ranking traditions.
